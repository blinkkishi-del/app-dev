## Introduction
Frieren: Beyond Journey's End is a fantasy anime series adapted from the manga written by Kanehito Yamada and illustrated by Tsukasa Abe.

## Synopsis
Because Frieren is an elf with a lifespan of thousands of years, a decade feels like a mere fraction of a moment to her. Decades later, when her former companions grow old and pass away, Frieren realizes how little she truly knew about them and regrets not spending more time connecting with humans. She embarks on a new travel route northward to the resting place of souls to speak with Himmel once more, learning the value of human connection, memories, and time along the way
