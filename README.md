# Frieren is an ancient, near-immortal elf mage who appears as a petite young woman with white hair, green eyes, and pointed ears. Despite her vast magical power and centuries of experience, she often acts like a carefree, sleepy child in her daily life.

**Personality & Quirks**

1. ==Aloof and Stoic==
   Frieren rarely shows big emotions on her face and speaks in a calm, flat tone. Because she lives so long, she initially struggles to understand how quickly human feelings and lives pass.
2. ==Lazy Daily Habits== 
  She is notoriously hard to wake up in the morning and often relies on her apprentice, Fern, to get her out of bed and dressed. She also loves sleeping in until noon whenever she has the chance.
3. ==Obsessed with Grimoires== 
  Her greatest hobby is collecting rare, obscure, and seemingly useless folk magic spells. She gets genuinely excited over spells like turning sweet grapes sour or removing rust from bronze statues.
4. ==Easily Tricked by Mimics== 
  Even though she is a brilliant mage who can detect almost any trap, she falls for chest-shaped monsters called Mimics nearly every single time. She always convinces herself there is a 1% chance a rare grimoire is hidden inside.
